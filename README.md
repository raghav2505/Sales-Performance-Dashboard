Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across product categories, customer segments, regions, payment methods, and shipping modes.

Objectives

* Analyze sales trends over time.
* Compare sales across product categories.
* Understand regional sales performance.
* Evaluate sales by customer segment and payment mode.
* Compare shipping modes and their contribution to sales.

Dashboard Features

* Regional filters for interactive analysis.
* Category-wise sales comparison.
* Sales distribution by customer segment.
* Sales analysis by payment mode and ship mode.
* Monthly sales trend visualizations.

* Microsoft Power BI Desktop
